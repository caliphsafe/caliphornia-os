Caliphornia OS 43 Build Patch: Admin + Music + Share

What this patch does:
- Expands admin control into a real control center.
- Adds account admin tools: create account, update role, grant Kiiku, grant access.
- Adds song admin tools: create/edit songs, audio paths, lock/share flags, status, project assignment.
- Adds experience/admin surfaces for apps, projects and products.
- Adds invite link and blast email admin records.
- Adds the Admin link to the Home Screen for caliph.safe@gmail.com and role admin/owner users.
- Restores Music as a central music app with Songs, Favorites, Projects and Share views.
- Lets favorites behave like a playlist with editable order.
- Adds share buttons to Music song rows.
- Adds a global Share Playing control so songs can be shared from individual apps once playing.
- Adds Share stats to the Stats app: top sharers, most shared songs, accepted transfers and accounts from Share.

Supabase:
Run supabase/migrations/010_admin_music_share_controls.sql after uploading these files.

Vercel:
No package-lock.json is included.
If you want email blasts to actually send, set EMAIL_PROVIDER_WEBHOOK_URL and EMAIL_FROM in Vercel. Without those, blast records are saved/queued for review.
