Caliphornia OS Proximity Share 43 Patch

Purpose:
- Remove the link-first receiver flow.
- Allow a receiver with no account to open the Caliphornia OS main page and receive based on proximity.
- Keep Share as a location-consented web proximity experience, not true native AirDrop.

How it works:
1. Sender signs in and opens /apps/share.
2. Sender chooses a song or full project.
3. Sender starts Share and allows location.
4. Receiver opens the public Caliphornia OS main page, no account needed.
5. If receiver allows location and is close to the sender, a Nearby Share Receive card appears.
6. Receiver taps Receive and is routed to /guest/[token].
7. Song shares grant 1 guest listen.
8. Project shares grant 1 guest listen per song.

Files included:
- app/page.tsx
- components/share/ProximityReceivePrompt.tsx
- components/share/ShareClient.tsx
- components/GlobalQuickActions.tsx
- app/api/share/start/route.ts
- app/api/nearby/receive/start/route.ts
- app/api/nearby/receive/candidates/route.ts
- app/api/nearby/receive/confirm/route.ts

Notes:
- Browser location permission is required on both sender and receiver.
- The backend stores rounded coordinates only in metadata and the share session expires quickly.
- Do not include package-lock.json.
